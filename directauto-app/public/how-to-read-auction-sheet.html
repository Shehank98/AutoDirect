How to Read an Auction Sheet

Every car sold at a Japanese auction comes with an auction sheet — an inspector's honest report of the car's condition. Here's what the key parts mean.

Overall grade

GradeMeaning
S / 6Brand new or almost new
5Excellent condition, very low mileage
4.5Great condition, minor wear
4Good, average wear for age
3.5 / 3Noticeable wear or small repairs needed
R / RARepaired accident history

Interior grade (A–D)

A letter grades the interior: A = clean, through to D = poor. So a "4.5 A" car is mechanically great with a clean interior.

The map diagram

A car outline marks damage with codes — e.g. A = scratch, U = dent, W = repaired/wavy panel, S = rust. Numbers next to them show severity (1 = small, 4 = large).
