Vocabulary

Common terms you'll see when buying imported vehicles.

Auction Grade
The overall condition score given by the auction inspector (e.g. 4.5, 5).
Chassis Number
The unique vehicle identifier / VIN used to trace the exact car.
Mileage (Odometer)
Distance the car has travelled, usually shown in kilometres.
Grade R / RA
A vehicle with recorded and repaired accident history.
CBU
Completely Built Unit — a fully assembled imported vehicle.
CIF
Cost, Insurance & Freight — the landed price before local duty.
Duty / Tax
Government import charges payable when clearing the vehicle.
Hybrid
A vehicle powered by both a petrol engine and an electric motor.
Drive Type
2WD (two-wheel drive) or 4WD/AWD (all-wheel drive).
Transmission
Automatic (AT) or Manual (MT) gearbox.
